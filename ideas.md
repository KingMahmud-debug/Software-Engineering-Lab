# BashaMate Design Exploration

## Three Distinct Directions

### 1. Courtyard Editorial

**Very Brief Intro:** A warm, human housing marketplace inspired by Bangladeshi courtyards, printed rental notices, and sunlit apartment interiors. It makes an uncertain housing search feel calm, credible, and community-led.

**Probability:** 0.037

### 2. Campus Wayfinding

**Very Brief Intro:** A crisp, utility-forward experience inspired by campus signage, maps, and transit information. It prioritizes fast scanning, practical clarity, and confident decision-making.

**Probability:** 0.062

### 3. Night Lantern Network

**Very Brief Intro:** A dark social-matching environment with small lantern-like points of light representing trusted connections. It feels intimate and private, with more emphasis on roommate chemistry than housing discovery.

**Probability:** 0.014

## Chosen Direction: Courtyard Editorial

### Design Movement

**Warm editorial marketplace** with subtle influences from contemporary South Asian hospitality and residential print ephemera. The product should feel like a trustworthy local guide rather than a generic property portal.

### Core Principles

1. **Trust is visible:** Verification, tenant-fit, saved homes, and compatibility appear as clear, calm signals rather than technical jargon.
2. **Browsing feels tactile:** Layered paper surfaces, soft grain, masonry-like listing rhythms, and image-led cards make exploration feel considered.
3. **Information has hierarchy:** Rent, location, and tenant preference are surfaced immediately; secondary detail appears only when useful.
4. **Community is optimistic:** Rounded forms, warm copy, and candid lifestyle signals make roommate matching feel human without becoming casual or childish.

### Color Philosophy

The visual base is **parchment cream** to create warmth and reduce the sterile feeling of marketplace interfaces. **Ink navy** carries crucial reading content and establishes credibility. **Basha green** is the signature trust color for verified states, location, and positive progress; its restraint keeps it meaningful. **Terracotta orange** is reserved for decisive moments—saving a home, sending an inquiry, or beginning a match—so action feels intentional rather than loud. Subtle marigold and moss surfaces create depth without gradients becoming a dominant visual device.

### Layout Paradigm

The site uses a **guided editorial rail** rather than a centered template. On large screens, a slim navigation column and a wide content field create an asymmetric composition; home sections alternate between a feature story, a staggered listing shelf, and a compact matching panel. On small screens, the rail becomes a compact top bar and a bottom action dock. Search controls form a visible “finding strip” that remains near the content instead of becoming a detached dashboard.

### Signature Elements

1. **Courtyard arches:** Soft arch frames for hero imagery, avatar stacks, and profile visual cues.
2. **Verification seal:** A two-part circular badge combining a check mark and a tiny house motif, repeated only for trusted entities.
3. **Field-note labels:** Slightly letter-spaced micro labels and dotted dividers inspired by annotated rental notebooks.

### Interaction Philosophy

Interactions should reinforce certainty. Filter changes immediately update the browse view, saving a listing feels like marking a page in a notebook, and compatibility is explained as readable reasons rather than a mysterious number. Buttons respond with a restrained press; overlays and drawers arrive from the user’s point of action. Frontend-only actions clearly disclose their demo status when a real backend would be required.

### Animation

Entry motion uses a short 220–280ms transform-and-opacity transition with a confident custom ease-out. Listing cards rise by a few pixels on hover, save marks animate with a single soft scale pulse, and the featured matching score fills only when its panel becomes visible. Navigation and filter feedback remain nearly instant. No essential content depends on animation, and every non-essential animation is disabled for users who prefer reduced motion.

### Typography System

**DM Serif Display** is used for high-impact display headings, listing names, and narrative moments; its editorial warmth makes BashaMate recognisable. **Manrope** is used for interface, filters, body text, metadata, and controls because its open forms remain legible at compact sizes. Headings use tight but breathable line-height, normal sentence case, and intentional italic emphasis only for personal or place-oriented language. Interface labels are compact uppercase with moderate tracking, never excessive.

### Brand Essence

**BashaMate helps bachelor students find a place—and the right people to share it with—through clearer housing signals and trusted local connections.**

**Personality:** Grounded, welcoming, assured.

### Brand Voice

The voice is direct, kind, and locally aware. Headlines make the next step feel possible; calls to action tell users exactly what will happen; microcopy explains trust without fear-based language.

> “Find a home that says yes to your next semester.”

> “Save the places that already feel like a good fit.”

### Wordmark & Logo

The mark is a **simple terracotta doorway nested within a green house outline**, forming a friendly monogram-like symbol without text. The wordmark pairs a compact Manrope “Basha” with a lightly italic DM Serif “Mate” to express both practical search and human connection. The standalone icon remains recognizable at favicon and header scale.

### Signature Brand Color

**Basha Green — #1F6B55.** It is the defining trust color used for verification, positive compatibility, and primary navigation accents.

## Style Decisions

The first delivery established the Courtyard Editorial visual language. The refinement keeps its warm, trustworthy palette and recognisable brand mark, but removes overly poetic or decorative language from product screens. The revised interface uses concise labels, clearer task-based headings, lighter page ornament, and practical information hierarchy. BashaMate is now framed as a **Bangladesh-wide rental and roommate search platform**, so search controls and sample data should reference multiple cities rather than a single Dhaka-area journey.

Utility screens retain the same product identity through at least one signature motif: field-note labels, dotted rental-note dividers, courtyard arches, or the BashaMate house-and-check verification seal. Basha Green remains the trust, verification, and selected-state color; terracotta is reserved for decisive actions such as sign-in, inquiries, matching, messages, and listing submission.
